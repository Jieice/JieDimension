# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Players, creators, collaborators, and followers who want to understand what JieDimension Studio is building, try playable web projects, and find the studio's social or contact channels.

## Product Purpose

JieDimension Studio's website presents the studio identity and project portfolio for AI agents, virtual interaction, independent development, and playable web games. Success means visitors can quickly understand the studio's direction, spot credible project work, and continue into a game, gallery, social channel, or contact path.

## Brand Personality

Experimental, hands-on, and playful. The site should feel like a small studio shipping real interactive work rather than a generic technology landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-SaaS landing page tropes, over-polished corporate agency language, heavy decorative neon, unreadable low-contrast overlays, and portfolio grids where every item has the same visual weight.

## Design Principles

- Show shipped artifacts before abstract claims.
- Keep the first screen dense enough to browse, but give priority projects room to breathe.
- Let game and tool imagery carry the brand instead of decorative effects.
- Make interaction paths obvious for visitors who want to play, follow, or contact.
- Preserve the independent-studio voice: direct, curious, and practical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Support keyboard focus, readable mobile text, reduced-motion preferences, and touch targets of at least 44px where practical.
